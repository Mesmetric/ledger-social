Ledger Social – Data Deletion

Ledger Social keeps no data on any server. Everything it stores is on the account owner's phone.

  1. Remove the app's access: in Instagram, go to Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites, and remove Ledger Social.
  2. Delete the stored data: uninstall Ledger Social from the phone, or clear its storage in Android Settings → Apps → Ledger Social → Storage → Clear data.

If you commented on or messaged @ledgerexplained and want anything removed, send a direct message to @ledgerexplained on Instagram and it will be deleted.
