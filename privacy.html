Ledger Social – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Ledger Social is a personal Android app used by the owner of the Instagram account @ledgerexplained to manage that account: publishing posts, reading statistics, and replying to comments and messages on that account's own posts.

What data the app uses

  • The connected Instagram account's profile, posts, statistics, comments and messages, read through Meta's official Instagram API.
  • Comments and messages that other people send to that account, only so the account owner can read and answer them.

Where the data is kept

All data stays on the account owner's own phone. The login token is stored encrypted on the device. There is no Ledger Social server and no database. Data is not sold, shared or used for advertising.

Who the data is sent to

Only to Instagram (Meta), through its official API. When a photo or video is published, the file is placed briefly in the owner's own GitHub repository so Instagram can download it, and it is deleted as soon as Instagram has it.
